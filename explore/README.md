Looking around before registering. Nothing in this folder had its questions fixed in advance.

**How to read "not evidence".** The two write-ups here open by saying that nothing in them is evidence, and several of the JSON files carry the status "Not registered, not evidence". That was the house rule as first written, and it overstates. A second reader pointed out that it was also unfair, because the look that it dismissed (the loop control) favours the authors of the edit. What is true is narrower: these are **weaker evidence** than the registered runs, because the questions were chosen along the way and nothing was held out. The files are left as they were written. This note, added on 13 November 2026, is the correction.
