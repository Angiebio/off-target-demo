# Findings

Nineteen findings, in the order they were reached. Each says what kind of evidence it rests on. Numbers that come from the article's data file carry their address and are checked by `python -m offtarget.verify`. Claims that rest on the authors' own files are listed with file and line in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

Kinds of evidence, strongest first for our own work:

- **weights**: our tools on the published tensors. Anyone with the two checkpoints can rerun them.
- **logs**: the authors' published log repository, read as data.
- **answers** and **scores**: the generations and per-problem marks the authors published.
- **registered**: a run of ours whose questions and thresholds were frozen first.
- **afterwards**: a table drawn from a registered run after its verdict was read. A description and a lead.
- **exploration**: looking around. Weaker evidence, because the questions were chosen along the way.

## An exploration worth naming

On <!--q:loop_control/n-->96<!--/q--> problems with thinking on and a limit of <!--q:loop_control/cap-->4,096<!--/q--> tokens, the edit took looping answers from <!--q:loop_control/arms/dose:0/loop-->42<!--/q--> to <!--q:loop_control/arms/dose:6/loop-->15<!--/q-->. Two random directions scaled to push as hard did not cut looping, and at the harder scale they left most answers in a loop (<!--q:loop_control/arms/loopmatched:0/loop-->77<!--/q--> and <!--q:loop_control/arms/loopmatched:1/loop-->73<!--/q-->). A limit that short under-counts loops, because most take longer to harden, and under the edit the loops that remained set in later. If the result holds at registered size and at the full limit, the benefit belongs to the direction, which supports the authors' claim with a harder control than the one they ran. `explore/loop-control-pilot-12NOV2026.md`.

## What the comparison in finding 17 is and is not

It sets two setups side by side. Ours used a different inference engine, one sample per problem where the authors took <!--q:default_mode/setup/theirs/samples_per_problem-->4<!--/q-->, and a limit of <!--q:default_mode/setup/ours/cap-->8,192<!--/q--> tokens where theirs was <!--q:default_mode/setup/theirs/cap-->32,768<!--/q-->. Our limit is the one in the authors' own non-thinking profile. The difference between the model's two settings in these runs is therefore a difference between configurations. It is not the measured effect of one switch.

## What would change our minds

1. **Both models in both settings on one engine**, with repeated samples and matched token budgets. If the unedited model with thinking off scores well below its figure in finding 17 there, that finding shrinks.
2. **The duplicate alarm test replicated on fresh labels and layouts**, with exact handoffs over all lists as the main outcome. If the harm does not replicate, finding 16 is a property of five labels.
3. **The loop control at registered size**, with eight random directions and the unedited model run against itself. If matched random pushes cut looping too, the benefit is not specific to the direction.
4. **The instruction question asked on fresh logs with several wordings of each condition.** If wording stops mattering, finding 18 is a property of four sentences.
